# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

CloudAI is committed to a welcoming, safe and harassment-free community for
everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient and constructive.
- Welcome different viewpoints and give actionable feedback.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Accept responsibility, correct mistakes and respect reasonable boundaries.
- Protect private information and obtain consent before sharing it.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking or sustained disruption.
- Discriminatory, insulting, sexualized or demeaning language or imagery.
- Personal or political attacks unrelated to the project.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberate abuse of project infrastructure or reporting channels.
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  community.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
commits and other project spaces. It also applies when someone officially
represents CloudAI in public.

## Reporting

For confidential reports, use the repository's
[private vulnerability report](https://github.com/liaovoxuan/CloudAI/security/advisories/new)
and prefix the title with `[Code of Conduct]`. Do not include sensitive incident
details in a public issue. If private reporting is unavailable, open a minimal
issue asking the maintainer for a private contact method without naming people
or describing the incident.

Maintainers will handle reports as privately and fairly as possible. A reporter
will not be penalized for a good-faith report.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity and history, maintainers may edit or remove content,
issue a private correction or warning, temporarily restrict participation, or
permanently ban a participant. Immediate restrictions may be applied when
needed to protect the community. Retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.

This policy is informed by the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
but is adapted for CloudAI's available reporting channels.
