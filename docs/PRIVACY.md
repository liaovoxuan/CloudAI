# Privacy Policy

CloudAI does not require a CloudAI account and does not collect analytics or
telemetry by itself.

When a user submits a prompt, image, or document, CloudAI sends the content
needed for that request to the AI service configured by the user. Processing,
retention, and security are governed by the selected provider's terms and
privacy policy. Do not send sensitive information unless you trust that
provider.

CloudAI may make user-initiated network requests and may check for software
updates when that option is enabled.
